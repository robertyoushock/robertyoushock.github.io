Our Places

A phone-first web app for two people to keep a top 10 in every food category, plus a wish list of places to try. It has a map with category pins, offline mode, drag to re-rank, and Spotify Wrapped-style story cards drawn on the phone. It runs for $0 a month.

Year
2026
Service
Product design, front-end, back-end
Client
Personal
Built in
Vanilla JS, Cloudflare Pages + D1, MapLibre

Problem

Two people, dozens of favorite spots, and the same question every weekend: where should we eat? Notes apps and map lists don't rank anything, don't sync cleanly between two phones, and don't remember what to order.

Solution

I designed and built a home-screen app where every category gets a ranked top 10 and a wish list, synced between both phones and usable with no signal. Design came first: two visually distinct lists, a food-icon map, and a warm type-driven look. The whole stack runs on free tiers with daily caps, so it can never send a bill.
