Earthquake Risk

For KREM's deep dive into Northwest earthquake risk, I built a suite of graphics, including a Google Earth Studio map and vector explainers, that made tectonic science engaging and easy to follow.

Year
2024
Service
Motion infographics, geospatial design
Client
KREM
Built in
After Effects, Google Earth Studio

Problem

Subduction zones, fault lines and volcanic activity feel abstract and overwhelming to most viewers, and charts or static images rarely capture the scale, urgency or geography.

Solution

I created a package that pairs Google Earth Studio mapping with custom vector diagrams of tectonic plates, earthquake terms and seismic hazards, bringing scientific depth to KREM's coverage while keeping it easy to understand.
