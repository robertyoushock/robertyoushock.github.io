Airbag Recall

A broadcast breakdown of why more than 457,000 Ford and Mazda vehicles were under urgent "Do Not Drive" orders over faulty Takata airbag inflators, combining geospatial visuals, motion-driven data layers and infographic storytelling.

Year
2024
Service
Motion infographic
Client
KARE 11
Built in
After Effects

Problem

It's the largest auto safety recall in U.S. history, yet millions of dangerous inflators remain unrepaired. The challenge was communicating that urgency clearly.

Solution

I built a visual package that layers animated geospatial overlays with infographics broken down by make, model and urgency, showing the scope, the human toll (27 deaths, 400+ injuries) and the free fix, with a clear call to check your VIN.
