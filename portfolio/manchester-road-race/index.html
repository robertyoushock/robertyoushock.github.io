Manchester Road Race Map

Run the course before the runners do. This animated map of the Thanksgiving Day Manchester Road Race, built in Google Earth Studio and After Effects, brought the event to life on WTIC's broadcast with dynamic movement and cinematic clarity.

Year
2023
Service
Geospatial design
Client
FOX61 (WTIC)
Built in
After Effects, Google Earth Studio

Problem

Static maps miss the excitement and detail of a live race, and leave viewers without a real sense of the course.

Solution

I independently built a fully animated course map that pairs accuracy with engaging motion: a broadcast-ready graphic that anchored WTIC's full-day coverage and kept viewers connected to the race.
