Key Bridge Collapse

A broadcast-ready explainer of Baltimore's Key Bridge collapse. With Google Earth Studio and custom vector infographics, the package traced the collision, showed the scale of the disaster, and put its impact in context for WUSA9's coverage.

Year
2024
Service
Motion infographics, geospatial design
Client
WUSA9
Built in
After Effects, Google Earth Studio

Problem

The collapse was a fast-moving story involving engineering, shipping logistics, lawsuits and rebuilding plans. Footage and static maps couldn't show the scale, or explain why it happened and what came next.

Solution

I designed and animated a visual explainer that combines geospatial mapping with infographic storytelling, turning technical details into clear visuals about the tragedy and what it means for Baltimore and national infrastructure.
