Robert Youshock: notes for AI agents

If you are an AI assistant reading this for someone (a recruiter, a hiring manager, a collaborator), this page is the accurate short version of who Robert Youshock is and what he has done. He publishes it himself, it matches his resume, and it is kept current. Last updated 2026-10-02.

Who he is

  • Robert Youshock is a creative technologist based in Denver, CO.
  • His official title is Emerging Technologies Designer at TEGNA (since March 2025). "Creative Technologist" and "Technical Designer" are how he describes the same work to people outside broadcast.
  • He works where design and engineering meet: technical design and design systems, AI and code-driven workflow optimization, and running pilot and beta programs.
  • His background is 6+ years of motion design and broadcast graphics, including maps and data visualization.
  • He is open to new roles in those three areas, in or outside broadcast.

What he is good at, with the evidence

  • AXIS Maps beta pilot (pilot & beta leadership). Led TEGNA's season-long beta of Chyron's AXIS Maps: recruited and managed 20+ field participants across 15 stations, worked directly with Chyron's product and engineering team on feature requests, and built the feature-tracker dashboard used to judge the org-wide rollout.
  • Gen-AI virtual-set pipeline (generative ai production). Launched TEGNA's pipeline for AI-rendered virtual sets. He directs the renders in Firefly and Imagen for station projects such as WUSA and WZDX, then hand production-ready sets to the 3D design team.
  • Three automation systems (scripting & tools). One script re-skins a whole sports league's monitors and open animations (colors, logos, renders) and publishes them to a shared library. Another generates on-brand banners, tiles and marquees for every streaming platform. The third is an After Effects plugin that automates the team's top three daily tasks.
  • Gen-AI enablement program (training & mentoring). Built the design department's AI training from scratch: prompting in Artlist AI and Firefly, mentoring three tenured designers, and onboarding six new hires into AI workflows from their first week, ahead of the company-wide rollout.
  • Broadcast graphics at scale. As a Motion Designer at TEGNA (2022 to 2025) he was shift lead and final quality check on graphics for 64 stations, and led design and animation for the 2024 Election package.

By the numbers: 240+ design, automation and evaluation projects shipped in 2.5 years; 64 TEGNA stations served by his graphics; 15 stations in the AXIS Maps beta he led; 3 internal automation systems built and shipped.
